Rumble Poster

Rumble Poster is a personal, non-commercial tool. It generates original physics simulation videos (ball races, ring escapes and ball battles) on the owner's computer and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts on a schedule, using the official TikTok Content Posting API and Instagram Graph API.

The tool is used only by its owner, to manage their own accounts. It does not offer accounts or services to the public, and it does not access other people's accounts or data.
