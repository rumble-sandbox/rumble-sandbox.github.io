Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Rumble Poster ("the App") is a personal tool that its owner uses to publish videos to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

Only for the owner's own accounts: basic profile information (account ID, username), the access tokens needed to upload videos, and statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) for videos published by the App.

How data is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to measure their performance. It is stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other users

The App does not collect any data about other TikTok or Instagram users.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from their TikTok or Instagram settings. All locally stored data can be deleted by removing the App's data folder.
